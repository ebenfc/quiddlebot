How to Use QuiddleBot

📝 Getting Started

  1. Select number of players (2-4)
  2. Enter player names
  3. For 2 players, optionally enable "Most Words" bonus
  4. Tap "Begin Quest" to start

🎮 Each Round

  1. Players calculate their own card points
  2. Enter each player's score in the app
  3. Select who won Longest Word bonus (or Tie/None)
  4. If enabled, select Most Words winner
  5. Tap "Next Round" to continue

★ Bonuses

Longest Word: +10 points to the player who spelled the longest word this round.

Most Words: +10 points to whoever made the most words (3+ players always, optional for 2).

Ties: If players tie for a bonus, select "Tie/None" - no bonus is awarded.

✏️ Editing & History

Tap any completed round dot to edit scores. Changes automatically recalculate totals.

Use the Menu to view all rounds or end the game early.

How to Play Quiddler

🎯 Objective

Score the most points by arranging your cards into words. Each card shows a letter and point value.

🃏 The Deck

118 cards with letters A-Z plus special double-letter cards:

QU, IN, ER, TH, CL

Each card has a point value (1-15 points).

📤 Dealing

Quiddler has 8 rounds. Cards dealt per round:

  • Round 1: 3 cards
  • Round 2: 4 cards
  • Round 3: 5 cards... and so on
  • Round 8: 10 cards

Remaining cards form a draw pile. Flip one card to start the discard pile.

🔄 Gameplay

On your turn:

  1. Draw one card from the draw pile OR take the top discard
  2. Discard one card to end your turn

Goal: Arrange all your cards into valid words.

🏁 Going Out

To go out, you must:

  • Use ALL cards in your hand to form words
  • Each word must be 2+ cards
  • Discard one final card

After someone goes out, other players get one more turn to form words with their cards.

📊 Scoring

  • Words: Add up point values of cards used
  • Unused cards: Subtract their points
  • Minimum: You can't score below 0 in a round

★ Round Bonuses

Longest Word: +10 points to whoever spelled the longest word

Most Words: +10 points to whoever made the most words

Same player can win both! Ties = no bonus awarded.

📚 Valid Words

  • Must be in your chosen dictionary
  • No proper nouns, abbreviations, or hyphens
  • Minimum 2 cards per word

🏆 Winning

After 8 rounds, the player with the highest total score wins!
